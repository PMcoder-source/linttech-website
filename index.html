Why It Matters

Lint is not just waste.
It is a liability.

🔥
Leading Cause of Dryer Fires
Lint accumulation in commercial dryer ducts is the primary ignition source for dryer fires in North American hospitality and healthcare facilities. Removing lint entirely eliminates the fuel load — not just manages it.
⚡
20–30% Energy Waste Per Load
Clogged lint filters force dryers to run significantly longer per cycle. Hotels and laundromats with regular lint removal run more efficient machines, reducing energy costs measurably over time.
🌊
Microfiber Pollution at Scale
US household dryers alone release over 3,500 metric tons of microfibers annually into the environment (DRI, 2025). Lint discarded in general waste contributes to microplastic contamination of soil and waterways.

Our Story

A Vancouver couple who got tired of watching good material go to waste.

We came to Vancouver from two different professional worlds — business strategy and the textile industry — and found ourselves asking the same question: why is something as continuously generated, as material-rich, and as problematic as dryer lint completely ignored by every recycling system in the country?

Preksha's background in fashion and textiles gave us a materials lens. Varun's background in business and systems thinking gave us the commercial lens. Together, we started looking at lint not as a nuisance but as a resource. What we found was a genuine gap — a waste stream generating 5,600 metric tons per year in Metro Vancouver alone, with zero recovery infrastructure and growing scientific interest in what it could become.

We are not scientists. We are two people who care deeply about the climate crisis and believe that solving the world's waste problems does not always require inventing something new — sometimes it just requires looking at what is already there and deciding it deserves better than a landfill.

"Every hotel towel that goes through a dryer leaves a little of itself behind. We want to give that something back to the world."
— Varun & Preksha, Co-Founders

Process

Four steps.
Zero disruption.

01
We provide a container
LintTech supplies a labelled collection bin for your laundry room. We install it next to your dryers. No purchase required.
02
Your team drops lint in
Housekeeping cleans filters as normal — they drop lint into our container instead of the bin. Same time. One different destination.
03
We collect on schedule
Free pickup on a weekly or biweekly schedule you choose. Each collection is weighed on-site and logged. Signed receipt same day.
04
You receive a diversion record
Every quarter we issue your Waste Collection Record — kg diverted, CO₂e estimate, collection log. Ready for LEED, Green Key, or ESG use.

Common Questions

Everything you want to know
before you call us.

Does this change our existing waste contract?
+
Not at all. Lint is set aside before it enters your general waste stream. Your waste contractor relationship stays completely unchanged. We only collect the lint.
What if you miss a scheduled pickup?
+
We always give 24-hour notice if we need to reschedule. We provide a container large enough to hold at least two weeks of lint. The 30-day cancellation clause means you can exit immediately if we fail to perform.
What do you actually do with the lint?
+
We are researching its potential as a recovered raw material — the science is promising and we are actively exploring what it can become. What we know for certain right now: it leaves your building and it does not go to landfill. We will share our research findings publicly as they develop.
Can we use your records in LEED or Green Key submissions?
+
Our records are based on exact weighed measurements and use ECCC National Inventory Report emissions factors. They are designed as useful supporting documentation for waste diversion credits. We recommend confirming with your specific certification body — we are happy to adapt the format if needed.
Is there any change to housekeeping workflow?
+
One change only: instead of dropping lint into the general bin, your team drops it into our labelled container. The time taken is identical. We have designed the program specifically so the ask on housekeeping is zero beyond that single action.
Who is LintTech and how long have you been operating?
+
LintTech Materials Inc. is a Vancouver startup founded by Varun and Preksha — a couple with backgrounds in business and the textile industry. We are currently incorporating as a BC company. We are being transparent about our early stage because partners deserve honesty from day one.
What does the program cost us?
+
Nothing. Collection is completely free. We provide the container, handle the collection, and issue the quarterly record at no cost. The only ask is a small space in your laundry room for our bin.
What if we want to stop participating?
+
30 days written notice, any time, no questions and no penalty. We collect our container and that is the end of it. We are building this on relationships, not contracts.
